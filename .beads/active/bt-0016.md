# Widget v2 — tappable tile grid (log today, every habit visible)

## Why

Dustin on bt-0011: "not great and not super usable." Diagnosis: I copied the app's week-grid row onto the Home Screen. With his 11 habits the medium widget shows 4 + "+7 more", and the only tap target is a 16pt square (Apple minimum 44). A widget's job is *log today without opening the app*; the week history lives in the app.

## Design (what Streaks / Reminders' widget do)

- Big tappable tiles, every habit visible, nothing scrolls. Medium = 8 cells (2×4), large = 12 (3×4) with the day summary on top. Small = ring + "N clean / N slips".
- Tile = `AppShape` square sized to fit the family (36–64pt, fit by height and width via GeometryReader): icon on a 14% tint of the habit colour, name under in `caption2`. Tap = `CompleteHabitIntent` (same rules as the app). Logged = solid colour (simple: habit colour; multi-state: success / partial / failed; avoidance slip: failed) with a luminance-chosen icon and a small badge in the corner (✓ / ◐ / ✕ on a system-background circle).
- Overflow: when habits exceed the cells, the last cell is a "+N / More" tile (Photos / Mail pattern); tapping it opens the app. No footer text.
- Same `AppTheme` palette, text styles, `.widgetAccentable()` on the coloured parts.
