# Dashboard rewrite — one day like the dh website, trends underneath

Design pass 2026-10-28. `bt-0022` (history) shipped 2026-10-28 evening,
so the data exists. The soft gate is **cleared**: dh-0019 (2026-10-28,
"Workout calories are Apple's Active Energy only") shipped and its
correction ran in production — Oct 13–28 sessions carry active numbers,
Aug 24's walk reads 483.8 (one record, not the 1189.8 double count).
**The dh website dashboard stays on until this passes side by side on
Dustin's phone.**

## Why

The Dashboard tab shows a rolling percentage and two streak tiles that read
0 most of the day. Today answers "what do I do now", the Journal "what
happened that day"; the Dashboard should answer **"am I slipping, and
where?"** — and now also do the dh website's job: one day's calories,
protein / carbs / fat against targets, the biggest-driver line, meals and
training.

Found in the code (2026-10-28), fixed here: "Best Streak" is the largest
*current* streak; the per-habit heatmap colours any entry, bypassing the
win rule (slips green, Tried and Rest full colour); "Days Completed" and
"Total Completions" are the same number; Best is range-limited while
Current looks back a year.

## Rules

- Everything in the day half comes from the Diet and Workout rows for that
  day (`HabitEntry.health`); today from the engine's live copy when the row
  has none yet. No network from the Dashboard. The iPad shows what iCloud
  carried.
- Before Oct 27 the nutrition block says "Meals start Oct 27"; Training
  shows dh's Workout history from Aug 1 (`bt-0022`).
- Calorie bar shows the band (floor and ceiling), not just the ceiling.
- Rings are the system `Gauge` in its circular style; carbs / fat over
  target turn the ring red, protein never does (website behaviour).
- Driver line = the website's rule: carbs or fat over target, the one
  further over by ratio, the meal with the most of that macro.
- Training rows: `active_kcal` null shows a dash; the dh website shows 0
  for the same case, so the side-by-side pass expects that one difference.
  Workout chips light from `activity_type` by a name map ("Indoor Run" and
  "Running" both → Cardio), never from `activity_code`.

### Calories burned (added 2026-10-28, after dh-0019)

- **Daily burn comes from Apple Health, read by Bullet itself like steps.**
  `HealthSteps` grows two more cumulative-sum queries per local day:
  Active Energy (`activeEnergyBurned`) and Resting Energy
  (`basalEnergyBurned`); total burned = the two added. The read-permission
  request grows to three types (the system shows its sheet again for the
  new two). Stored beside the day's steps on the Workout row's
  `HealthSnapshot` (`activeBurnKcal` / `restingBurnKcal`, both optional,
  missing key decodes as `nil`), written through `HabitStore.setHealth`
  only when changed, iPhone only; the iPad shows what iCloud carried.
  The Dashboard day view shows **Eaten** and **Burned** side by side.
  Dustin's Fitness check for Oct 27: Move 1,030 active, 3,189 total — the
  pass test reads those two numbers back from the app.
- **The rule: daily burned = Apple Health's daily total, nothing else.**
  dh's per-session `active_kcal` is shown per workout row and is **never
  added to the daily burn** — the Health total already contains it.
  `HealthSnapshot.totalActiveKcal` (the session sum) feeds the check
  below and nothing else.
- **The check, never silent:** when a day's session `active_kcal` sum is
  greater than that day's Apple Health Active Energy, the Training block
  shows a warning line with both numbers ("Sessions add up to 612 active
  cal; Apple Health counted 480 for the day"). Skipped when either side
  is unknown. Dash, not 0, when Health has no reading.
- **Refetch once, Aug 1 → today.** Stored `health` blocks only refresh
  through the engine's `daysToGrade`: the 7-day lookback plus dh's
  changed days, and changed days **before the Oct 27 go-live are
  dropped**, so the corrected Aug–Oct 26 sessions can never refresh on
  their own. One-time pass, iPhone + key only, the `bt-0022` pattern:
  a Settings → Data Management row ("Refresh Workout Calories") that
  shows counts before and after and hides itself via `@AppStorage` once
  run. It pulls dh facts Aug 1 → today (89 days, one range under the
  93-day cap), rebuilds each Workout row's `HealthSnapshot` from the new
  sessions, reads that day's steps + active + resting from HealthKit,
  and writes through `setHealth` — grade, source and edit time untouched,
  rows without sessions untouched. Evidence on the screen: sessions
  refreshed, sessions that changed, and Aug 24's walk before / after
  (1189.8 → 483.8).
- **Side by side with the dh website, two expected differences and no
  others:** a missing number shows a dash in Bullet and 0 on the website;
  Aug 24 reads 483.8 in Bullet and 1189.8 on the website.
- Charts are Swift Charts (first use in the app). Follow the dataviz
  skill before writing chart code.
- Trends: week bar of habits won per day with a comparison to the previous
  period; habit rows in Today order with a mini 7 / 30-day strip
  (`DaySquare` family), count, trend arrow, streak; nutrition and training
  trend from stored rows. The three stat tiles go. Streaks start from
  yesterday while today is open.
- Detail page: heatmap through the win rule (positive habits; `bt-0024`
  already draws an avoidance habit's slips in red), Best over the
  lookback, duplicate row removed, one range control, Edit in the toolbar.
- From `bt-0024` (2026-10-28): avoidance habits have **no streak and no
  win**; their rate is a slip rate ("N% slipped · N days"); in the week
  bar a slip is a mark on the day, not a point lost; "X of N" counts
  positive habits only. The detail page's default 12-week window counts
  days before the Oct 27 clean start in its denominator (Weed "0 of 84
  days"): the one range control should start at the habit's clean start
  / go-live, and streaks should never walk back past it.
