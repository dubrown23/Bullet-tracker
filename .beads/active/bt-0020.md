# Send hand taps to dh automatically — so "what's left today" by voice knows what I tapped

## Why

Step 3 (`bt-0019`, shipped 2026-10-27) sends today's hand ticks to dustin-health as part of every sync, so Claude voice's "what's left today" can skip what Dustin already tapped. But a sync only runs when the app opens or when he pulls down on Today. In the pass test he tapped Pills, asked "what's left today", and Pills was still listed — the tap hadn't travelled yet. He had to pull down first.

Wanted (Dustin, 2026-10-27): after any hand tap — on Today, in Log Details, or on the widget — the iPhone sends a **quiet sync a few seconds later**, and several taps in a row make **one** sync, not one each. iPhone only, same rule as grading and the rest of the sync; the iPad never calls dh.

Proposed scope_files above are a guess at where the trigger lands (the write path in `HabitStore`, the engine's debounce, the widget's own write); locked at go, after the plan.

## Out of scope

Background refresh or silent push from dh (parked in the shared note: only if the lag annoys him); syncing from the iPad; sending anything other than what `op=sync` already carries; changing how dh answers "what's left today".
