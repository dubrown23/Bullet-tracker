# Bullet copies Apple Watch steps + Oura sleep to dh

Spec from Dustin 2026-11-10 (next phase of dustin-system in_flight
`dh-bullet-health-pipe-2026-11-09.md`); dh's receiving side is live
(`dh-0034`, same `op=health`).

## Why

Voice must never read Apple Health (the Claude app then turns off dh for the
chat), so Bullet copies what voice needs. The proof the same morning (debug
screen, read-only) showed: Pedometer++ writes nothing to Health — it shows
the Watch's steps (Nov 9: Watch 11,871, Pedometer++ 11,877, Health total
11,880); Oura is the only sleep source (`com.ouraring.oura`) and its samples
overlap in Health, so a plain sum over-counts (13h 15m in an 8h 41m window);
counted once, Nov 9 = 7h 47m vs Oura app 7h 46m.

## Plan

- `POST ?op=health` gains `steps` and `sleep` lists beside `workouts` /
  `samples`; each list ≤ 400 per send; any list alone.
- Steps: Apple Watch only (device model "Watch"), Health's day sum, whole
  number; `counted_at` = read time. A day with no Watch steps is not sent.
- Workout habit's Partial grade reads the same Watch-only count
  (`HealthSteps.steps(on:)`), so one day never has two step numbers.
- Sleep: Oura only. Sessions split on > 2 h gaps; naps (start ≥ 10:00 and
  wake ≤ 20:00 the same day) left out; pieces filed under the day of wake
  (+6 h, so a 23:50 wake joins the next morning) merge into one night: first
  bedtime, last wake, stages added. Each minute counted once: a minute in two
  stages goes to deep > REM > core > awake (asleep total kept; "asleep
  unspecified" counts as core). Whole minutes by largest remainder so the
  four add up to the rounded total.
- When: every app open and every HealthKit background wake (workout or
  sleep observer). Each run sends Watch steps for yesterday + today and Oura
  nights woken on in the last 3 days. Backlog once: every day / night Health
  has, back up to 400 days; "Send all again" repeats it.
- Settings → Dustin's Health: steps saved / older ignored, sleep saved /
  unchanged, refusals with their day.
- The debug proof screen comes out; its night logic moves to
  `Sync/HealthDaily.swift` with its tests.

## Out of scope

- Steps / sleep from any other source; any fallback when the Watch misses a
  day.
- Naps.
- Health permission wording (project file; hook-blocked).

## Pass

Phone: Settings shows steps + sleep saved; dh's Nov 9 = 11,871 steps and the
Nov 10 night ≈ 7h 47m asleep; Workout's step caption matches the Watch.
