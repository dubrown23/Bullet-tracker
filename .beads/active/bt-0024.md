# Avoidance habits — only slips get marked

Design pass 2026-10-28 (this file is the record). Waiting on Dustin's go
and his answer to the one question below.

## Why

Dustin's decision after the `bt-0022` phone pass, replacing "stop the
streak at Oct 27": for an avoidance habit (Weed, Alcohol, Poison) **only
slips get marked**. A clean day shows nothing: no green square, no streak
flame. The wipe exposed the old reading — Weed shows a 365-day streak
because `HabitWinRule` (bt-0014) counts a day with no row as a win and
the streak walks back a year — but the reading was wrong before the wipe
too: a year of "wins" for not doing something is not the picture he wants.

```
Weed — Mon Tue Wed Thu Fri Sat Sun
Now:   ■   ■   ✕   ■   ■   ■   ■   🔥 365
Want:  ·   ·   ✕   ·   ·   ·   ·
```

Not waiting for the Dashboard rewrite (`bt-0023`): this is its own small
bead, and `bt-0023` builds on it.

where `.avoidance` returns true for a past day with no row. These are the
places that reading surfaces, and what each becomes.

`isNeutral`, the history rewrite.

## Dustin's answer (2026-10-28): a bad day counts against the percentage, like a good habit's score

Neither A nor B. **Percentages stay a real score**: a slip costs an
avoidance habit exactly what a missed day costs a good habit. Clean days
over tracked days, same fraction, same bar. Weed with one slip in October
reads **30 of 31 · 97%**, not "1 slip" and not 100%.

So the table above changes in two rows:

- **#6 detail page** — Completion Rate / Days Completed stay (30 of 31,
  97%); the **heatmap** still marks slips in red and clean days grey; the
  Streaks section is hidden; "Total Completions" (the duplicate) goes.
- **#7 Dashboard row / #8 PDF line** — keep the percentage and the bar;
  nothing reads "N slips" in place of a score. The streak tiles and the
  PDF best-streak still skip avoidance habits.

What changes is only the **picture of a day**: the grid shows a slip and
nothing else, no flame, no streaks. The header tail stays as it is
("· 3 clean" / "· 1 slip") — it is already a score-shaped line and the
"X of 8" number still leaves avoidance out (bt-0014).

Pass test adjusts to match: Dashboard → Weed "97%" with the bar; detail
→ "30 of 31", heatmap red on the slip day only, no Streaks section.

Waiting on go.
