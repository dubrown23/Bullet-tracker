# Avoidance habits — only slips get marked

Design pass 2026-10-28 (this file is the record). Waiting on Dustin's go
and his answer to the one question below.

## Why

Dustin's decision after the `bt-0022` phone pass, replacing "stop the
streak at Oct 27": for an avoidance habit (Weed, Alcohol, Poison) **only
slips get marked**. A clean day shows nothing: no green square, no streak
flame. The wipe exposed the old reading — Weed shows a 365-day streak
because `HabitWinRule` (bt-0014) counts a day with no row as a win and
the streak walks back a year — but the reading was wrong before the wipe
too: a year of "wins" for not doing something is not the picture he wants.

```
Weed — Mon Tue Wed Thu Fri Sat Sun
Now:   ■   ■   ✕   ■   ■   ■   ■   🔥 365
Want:  ·   ·   ✕   ·   ·   ·   ·
```

Not waiting for the Dashboard rewrite (`bt-0023`): this is its own small
bead, and `bt-0023` builds on it.

## Dustin's answer (2026-10-28): a slip counts toward a percentage the way a done day counts for a good habit

Neither A nor B. The percentage is a **slip rate**: slip days over scored
days, the mirror of a good habit's done rate.

```
Oct (30 days)
Workout  done on 20 days    → 67%  (higher is better)
Weed     slipped on 3 days  → 10%  (lower is better)
```

(A first misreading — "clean days over tracked days, 97%" — was written
here and is withdrawn. 97% would be a success rate; he wants the slips
counted, never the clean days.)

So for avoidance habits:

- **Percentage = slip days / scored days**, shown everywhere a good habit
  shows its rate: the Dashboard row (#7), the detail page (#6), the PDF
  summary (#8), and later the `bt-0023` rewrite. **Labelled as a slip
  rate** — "10% slipped" — never as a success rate; the bar fills with
  the slip rate in the failed red, so a long bar reads as bad. "Days
  Completed" on the detail page becomes "Slipped · 3 of 30 days".
- **Clean days are still never marked**: empty square (#1), no streak
  (#2), **no "· 3 clean" tail in the header** (#3, and the widget's copy
  of the line, #4). A slip day can read "· 1 slip".
- **Everything else as option A**: no streaks for avoidance anywhere (#2,
  #6 Streaks section hidden, #7 streak tiles and #8 PDF best-streak skip
  them); "X of N done" counts positive habits only (already so); in the
  `bt-0023` week bar a slip shows as a mark on the day, not a point lost.
- Heatmap (#6): slips in the failed red, clean days grey. Overall
  Completion % on the Dashboard and the PDF counts positive habits only
  (an avoidance habit has no done days to add to either side).

Mechanism unchanged from #9: one switch in `HabitWinRule`; the rate
functions return (slipDays, scoredDays) for avoidance and (doneDays,
expectedDays) for the rest; every reader labels by style.

**Go (Dustin, 2026-10-28):** keep the overall Completion % to positive
habits only — a slip never lowers it. One addition: **wherever a slip
rate shows, the number of slip days shows beside it** — "Weed · 10%
slipped · 3 days" on the Dashboard row, the detail page and the PDF.
